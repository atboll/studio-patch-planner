What is drawn

  • 19 inch wide, 3U high (482.6 × 133.4 mm), D-size cut-outs at 26 mm pitch, 16 per row.
  • Rows 1 and 2: mic lines 1–16 and 17–32 on female XLR.
  • Row 3: returns 1–8 on male XLR, then 8 blanked positions for later use.
  • Two label lines over each row, the same idea as on the patchbay: group name, then the number.
